Our Story

Born from a love
of light & story.

Project Stardust was founded in 2014 by a group of cinematographers and storytellers who believed that every brand has a film waiting to be made — one that moves, inspires, and endures.

Over a decade later, we've grown into a full-service production house trusted by global brands, independent artists, and mission-driven organizations alike. Our work has screened at festivals, aired on broadcast networks, and won international recognition.

What We Believe

Our philosophy.

✦

Craft Over Convenience

We take the longer, harder path if it means a better result. Every decision is made in service of the story, not the schedule.

◎

Intention in Every Frame

We don't shoot to fill time. Every frame has a reason, every cut has a purpose. Visual language is our first language.

⬡

Collaboration as Currency

The best films are made together. We treat every client as a creative partner and every crew member as an essential voice.
